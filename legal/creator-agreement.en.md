## 4. Revenue Share

### 4.1 The Split

AgentPick operates on a **70/30 revenue share model**:

- **70%** — paid to the Creator
- **30%** — retained by AgentPick (platform fee, infrastructure, payment processing, support)

This split applies to all net revenue attributable to your Creator Content, after deduction of payment processing fees charged by our payment provider (typically 2-3%).

**Example:** If your content generates €100 in gross subscription revenue in a month, and payment processing fees are €3, net revenue is €97. You receive 70% of €97 = **€67.90**.

### 4.2 Attribution Model

Revenue is attributed to creators based on platform usage metrics (e.g., number of active subscribers using a particular agent or skill during the billing period). The specific attribution formula will be published in the Creator Dashboard and may be updated with 30 days' notice.

### 4.3 Payment Schedule

Payments are made **monthly**, within **15 business days** after the end of each calendar month. For example, revenue earned in March will be paid by approximately April 21.

Payments will be made to the bank account or payment details you have registered in your Creator Dashboard.

### 4.4 Minimum Payout Threshold

If your monthly earnings are below **€20**, the amount will be rolled over to the following month until the threshold is reached.

### 4.5 Taxes

You are solely responsible for any taxes applicable to your creator income (including VAT, income tax, or withholding tax) in your country of residence or operation. AgentPick may be required by law to collect certain information for tax reporting purposes. We reserve the right to withhold amounts required by applicable law.

If you are based in the EU and are VAT-registered, you must provide your valid VAT number. If you are not VAT-registered, we will apply the reverse charge mechanism or other applicable rules.

### 4.6 Disputed Payments

If you believe a payment is incorrect, notify us at legal@agentpick.co within **30 days** of the payment date. We will investigate and correct any errors in good faith.

All Creator Content published on AgentPick must meet the following standards.

- Productivity and automation AI agents
